Frequently asked questions.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Stuart, FL?

Most interior rooms range from a few hundred to over a thousand dollars depending on size and prep work, while full exterior repaints typically run a few thousand dollars. Scollo Painting provides a fixed, written quote after an in-person estimate, so you know the total cost before work begins.

Is Scollo Painting Inc. licensed and insured?

Yes. Scollo Painting Inc. is fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial painting throughout Martin and Palm Beach County, Florida.
